# US-21: Research Paper Evaluation Section

**Phase:** 4 — Evaluation & Paper
**Reference:** [design-doc.md §14 Phase 4](../design-doc.md#phase-4-evaluation--paper-weeks-78)

## Story
As a **researcher**, I want a drafted evaluation section for the research paper so that the platform's contributions are publishable.

## Acceptance Criteria
- AC1: A draft `paper/evaluation.tex` (or `.md`) is written covering: research questions, study design, metrics, results, threats to validity, discussion.
- AC2: Each of the five metrics from [US-20](US-20-metrics-collection.md) is reported with supporting figures or tables.
- AC3: The draft cites relevant prior work on multi-agent planning, A2A protocol, and human-AI teaming.
- AC4: Limitations explicitly include: sample size (15–16), single-arm study, reference-agent simplicity.
- AC5: The draft is reviewed by at least one co-author and revised once before submission.
- AC6: All figures are reproducible from the analysis scripts committed in [US-20](US-20-metrics-collection.md).

## Out of Scope
- Introduction, related work, and system-description sections (handled separately).
- Conference / journal submission logistics.
- Rebuttal / camera-ready revisions.
- Author ordering or affiliation negotiations.
